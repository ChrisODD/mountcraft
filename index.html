Before you join

Choose your version.

Mount & Craft runs on a custom modpack, installed through Technic Launcher or CurseForge.

  1. Get a launcher

    Download and install the free Technic Launcher or the CurseForge app, then sign in with your Minecraft account.

    Get Technic LauncherGet CurseForge

  2. Install Mount & Craft

    Open the Mount & Craft modpack page and click Install, or search “Mount & Craft” in your launcher’s Modpacks tab.

    Download on TechnicDownload on CurseForge

  3. Launch and join

    Hit Play on the modpack in your launcher. The server opens soon, so follow our socials for updates.

If you play, always launch Minecraft from the Mount & Craft modpack in Technic Launcher or the CurseForge app. Keep the modpack updated in the launcher when a new version comes out, or the server will turn you away.

How to play

One sword is a skirmish. An army is a war.

Mount & Craft is a geopolitical sandbox. You play alongside other people, and the land is won by whoever brings the better army.

  1. Raise a warband

    Recruit followers and build a fighting force that follows you into the field.

  2. Drill them

    Train recruits into real soldiers: infantry, archers and cavalry who hold a line.

  3. Join a faction

    Sign up with an existing faction, or found your own and set its banner.

  4. Conquer settlements

    March your army on towns and castles, take them and hold them against rivals.

Play together

Factions are built from real players: allies, rivals and the politics between them.

Command, don’t just fight

Formation and numbers decide battles. Lone heroes get overrun.

Make your own place

Pick a spot in the geopolitical map and carve out your own power.

The mod

Ride, fight, hold the line.

The feel of a medieval battlefield inside Minecraft: horses under you, steel in hand, a drilled army at your back and a world that is always at war.

  • Mounted combat

    Charge in on horseback with a lance, or dismount and fight on foot with sword and shield.

  • Armour and arms

    Chainmail, plated shoulders and helms, with spears, maces, swords and bows to go with them.

  • Castles and towns

    Walled towns with gatehouses, stables and training grounds with archery targets.

  • A world at war

    Rival factions with their own towns, castles and armies, and the wars between them.
