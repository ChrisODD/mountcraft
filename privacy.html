Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mount & Craft collects when you visit mountandcraft.com, play on the Mount & Craft Minecraft server at play.mountandcraft.com, or interact with our community channels. Mount & Craft is an unofficial fan project run by independent creators.

1. Information we collect

  • Website visits. The website is hosted on Netlify. Netlify's servers may record standard request data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver and secure the site. We do not add our own analytics or advertising trackers to the website.
  • Gameplay on the server. When you join the server, the game needs your Minecraft username and UUID. The server also records what is needed to run the game, such as your connection address, play time, and in-game actions like kills, settlements and faction membership.
  • Community channels. If you join our Discord server or Telegram channel, or follow us on Instagram or TikTok, the platform handles the information you share there under its own rules.
  • Support. If you choose to support the project through Ko-fi, Ko-fi collects your payment and contact details. We receive only what Ko-fi passes on to us, such as your name or message if you include one.

2. How we use information

  • To run the website and keep the server working, fair and secure.
  • To keep the community informed about updates, events and server changes.
  • To detect and deal with cheating, abuse or breaches of the server rules.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

The website itself does not set tracking cookies. Netlify may set technical cookies needed to serve the site, and the third-party services linked from the site (Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Telegram and Ko-fi) may set their own cookies when you visit them.

4. Third-party services

Links on this site take you to services we do not control, including Netlify, Technic Launcher, Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Telegram and Ko-fi. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site. The live web map linked from the site is hosted separately and may log requests in the same way as any web server.

5. How long we keep information

Server records are kept for as long as needed to run the server and to handle disputes or rule breaches, then deleted or anonymised. Website hosting logs are kept according to Netlify's retention settings.

6. Your choices and rights

You can stop playing on the server at any time, leave our community channels, and ask us what personal information we hold about your Minecraft account. You can also ask us to correct or delete it, subject to the needs of running the server. To make a request, contact us through the Discord server or Telegram channel linked in the footer.

7. Children

The server is intended for players who meet the age requirements of the platforms they use. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before giving any personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and continued use of the website or server after a change means you accept the updated policy.
