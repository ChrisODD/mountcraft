Frequently asked questions about Mount & Craft.

Is it Bedrock or Java?

It is currently Java. If you want to support this mod to reach its peak, you can donate.

How do I install the modpack?

Install the Technic Launcher or the CurseForge app, then search for “Mount & Craft” in the Modpacks tab. Full steps are in the install section.

Where is the player's guide?

The player's guide walks through the controls, your warband, companion inventories, combat, the campaign, kingdoms, governors, sieges, family, faith, land and the sea.

Where can I get help or talk to other players?

Join our Discord.
